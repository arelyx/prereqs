## Course Requirements
Complete the following courses. Courses may be taken for a letter grade or Pass/No Pass.

### Lower-Division Courses {sc1}

#### Either six courses in the regular track {sc2}
- SPAN 1 — First-Year Spanish (5)
- SPAN 2 — First-Year Spanish (5)
- SPAN 3 — First-Year Spanish (5)
- SPAN 4 — Second-Year Spanish (5)
- SPAN 5 — Second-Year Spanish (5)
- SPAN 6 — Second-Year Spanish (5)
> NOTE: Or equivalent proficiency. SPAN 5M may substitute for SPAN 5.

#### Or three courses in the Spanish for Heritage Speakers (SPHS) track {sc2}
- SPHS 4 — Spanish for Heritage Speakers (5)
- SPHS 5 — Spanish for Heritage Speakers (5)
- SPHS 6 — Spanish for Heritage Speakers (5)
> NOTE: or equivalent proficiency

#### Plus the following course {sc2}
- LING 50 — Introduction to Linguistics (5)

### Upper-Division Courses {sc1}
There are a total of 25 upper-division credits required for the Spanish studies minor. Four required Spanish studies core courses (20 credits total), in addition to one upper-division Spanish studies elective course (5 credits total):

#### Literature (5 credits) {sc2}
Choose one of the following courses:
- LIT 189A — De la conquista a Sor Juana (5)
- LIT 189B — El Siglo XIX en América Latina: cultura, política y sociedad (5)

#### Spanish Studies (5 credits) {sc2}
- LIT 189C [/SPAN 105] — Introducción a Spanish Studies (5)

#### Linguistics (5 credits) {sc2}
- SPAN 150 — Topics in Hispanic Linguistics: Introduction to Hispanic Linguistics (5)

#### Spanish Language (5 credits) {sc2}
Choose one of the following courses:
- SPAN 114 — Advanced Conversation and Composition (5)
- SPHS 115 — El ensayo lectura, análisis y redacción (5)
