# Bioinformatics Minor

<!-- slug: bioinformatics-minor | edition: 2026-27 | url: https://catalog.ucsc.edu/en/current/general-catalog/academic-units/baskin-engineering/biomolecular-engineering/bioinformatics-minor -->
The bioinformatics minor is intended primarily for bioinformatics tool users who are majoring in a biological or chemical specialty. The bioinformatics minor is also appropriate for computer science or computer engineering majors who are considering graduate work in bioinformatics. Due to course overlap, the bioinformatics minor cannot be combined with the biomolecular engineering and bioinformatics major (BMEB) B.S. or biotechnology B.A.

### Upper-Division Courses {sc1}

#### Genetics {sc2}
Choose one of the following:
- BME 105 — Genetics in the Genomics Era (5)
- BIOL 105 — Genetics (5)
- METX 140 — Molecular Biology and Microbial Genetics (5)
> NOTE: BME 105: strongly recommended

#### Programming {sc2}
- BME 160 — Research Programming in the Life Sciences (6)
- BME 163 — Applied Visualization and Analysis of Scientific Data (5)
> NOTE: Students may substitute CSE 20 for BME 160, although BME 160 is strongly recommended. CSE 20 has a test-out exam that will also be accepted.

#### Statistics {sc2}

- ⟨or this course⟩
- STAT 131 — Introduction to Probability Theory (5)
> NOTE: STAT 131: see prerequisites.

#### Bioinformatics {sc2}
