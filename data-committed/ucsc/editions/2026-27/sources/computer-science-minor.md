## Course Requirements
Thirteen courses are required for the computer science minor. Some courses may be satisfied via [exam credit](https://catalog.ucsc.edu/en/current/general-catalog/undergraduate-information/undergraduate-academic-program/exam-credit). Though courses for the minor may be taken for a letter grade or Pass/No Pass, many of the courses may be required for a student's major which could require a letter grade (including all Baskin Engineering majors). Students should be aware of the letter grade policy for their chosen major before choosing Pass/No Pass

#### Plus two additional upper-division courses {sc2}
Two additional courses satisfying one of the following conditions. Lecture/lab combinations count as one course. If a lecture has a lab offered (required or optional), the lab must be passed to count for this requirement.
1. Any 5-credit or more upper-division CSE course with a number between 100 and 189 or CSE 195.
2. Any 5-credit or more CSE course with a number between 201 and 279. Approval for courses with numbers 290 and above may be requested by submitting a course substitution petition to the BE Undergraduate Advising Office. Courses with numbers 280-289 are not eligible. Undergraduate students require permission from the instructor to enroll in graduate courses.
3. Any course from the following list
- AM 148 — GPU Programming for Scientific Computations (5)
- AM 160 — Introduction to Scientific Machine Learning (5)
