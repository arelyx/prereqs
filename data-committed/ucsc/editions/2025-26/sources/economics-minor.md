## Course Requirements
Students earn a minor in economics by completing the following requirements. Courses may be taken for a letter grade or pass/no pass, but any courses taken pass/no pass cannot later be used toward an economics major since majors require letter grades. For that reason, taking courses for a letter grade is encouraged, but not required.

### Upper-Division Courses {sc1}

#### Choose one of the following courses: {sc2}
- ECON 100A — Intermediate Microeconomics (5)
- ECON 100M — Intermediate Microeconomics, Math Intensive (5)

#### Plus one of the following courses: {sc2}
- ECON 100B — Intermediate Macroeconomics (5)
- ECON 100N — Intermediate Macroeconomics, Math Intensive (5)

#### Plus the following course: {sc2}
- ECON 113 — Introduction to Econometrics (5)

#### Plus three economics electives {sc2}
Students complete three additional 5-credit electives chosen from ECON 100-189, excluding ECON 104. No course may satisfy more than one requirement of the minor.
