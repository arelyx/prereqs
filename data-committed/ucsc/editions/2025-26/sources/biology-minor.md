# Biology Minor

<!-- slug: biology-minor | edition: 2025-26 | url: https://catalog.ucsc.edu/en/2025-2026/general-catalog/academic-units/physical-and-biological-sciences-division/molecular-cell-and-developmental-biology/biology-minor -->
The biology minor is designed to incorporate elements of both the ecology and evolutionary biology discipline and the molecular, cell and developmental biology discipline.

## Course Requirements
Complete the following requirements. Courses must be taken for a letter grade. Some courses may be satisfied via [exam credit.](https://catalog.ucsc.edu/en/current/general-catalog/undergraduate-information/undergraduate-academic-program/ap-ibh-and-a-level-examinations/)

### Lower-Division Courses {sc1}

#### All of the following courses {sc2}
- BIOL 20A — Cell and Molecular Biology (5)
- BIOL 20L — Experimental Biology Laboratory (2)
- BIOE 20B — Development and Physiology (5)
- CHEM 8A — Organic Chemistry (5)
- CHEM 8B — Organic Chemistry (5)

#### Plus the following courses: {sc2}
- CHEM 3A — General Chemistry (5)
- CHEM 3B — General Chemistry (3)
- CHEM 3C — General Chemistry (3)
> NOTE: Note: This requirement may also be satisfied with prior completion of CHEM 1A, CHEM 1B, and CHEM 1C or equivalent. Previous to July 1, 2023, the General Chemistry series, offered by the Department of Chemistry and Biochemistry, was listed in the General Catalog as Chemistry 1A, 1B and 1C, with associated laboratories Chemistry 1M and 1N. As of academic year 2023-24, this series was replaced with two distinct series: Chemistry 3A, 3B and 3C, with labs Chemistry 3BL and 3CL; or Chemistry 4A and 4B with labs Chemistry 4AL and BL. Descriptions of these two series are provided here.

### Upper-Division Courses {sc1}

#### All of the following courses: {sc2}
- BIOL 100 — Biochemistry (5)
- BIOL 105 — Genetics (5)
- BIOE 107 — Ecology (5)
- BIOE 109 — Evolution (5)

#### Upper-Division Elective {sc2}
Students complete one upper-division elective of five credits or more chosen from BIOE 100-181 or BIOL 100-181.
