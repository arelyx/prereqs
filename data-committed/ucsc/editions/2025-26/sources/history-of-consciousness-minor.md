## Course Requirements

### Lower-Division Courses {sc1}
Students take one of the following courses. Students may petition for approval of an alternative departmental lower-division course.
- HISC 1 — Introduction to History of Consciousness (5)
- HISC 12 — Historical Introduction to Philosophy (5)

### Upper-Division Courses {sc1}
Students are required to take five 5-credit, upper-division history of consciousness courses numbered HISC 100-199.
Students may, with permission of the instructor, petition the department to substitute a 5-credit graduate seminar for one of the required upper-division courses. In addition, students may petition to substitute an upper-division course offered by affiliated faculty in other departments.
Students must complete all requirements for the minor with a grade of P, C (2.0), or better.
